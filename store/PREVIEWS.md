# App Store previews

Five plates, generated by `node store/previews.js` from the raw captures in
`store/screenshots/` plus `store/previews-mark.png`. Output lands in
`store/previews/`, which is what `store/asc.mjs screenshots` should upload.

Rerun the generator after any capture changes; never retouch the PNGs by hand.

---

## The thesis

**An engineer's drawing sheet, issued as five plates.**

Steve is a P.E. The logo is a goat planted on rock, which is a claim about
footing, not about luxury. The brand colours are engineering-ink navy and
surveyor's brass. So each panel is a numbered plate: a full-bleed rule with an
index and a role, small-caps mono annotations, and the phone cropped like a
detail view running off the bottom edge.

This is not a generic "dark background with a floating phone" treatment. Every
decision below traces back to that sentence.

## Reference points

| Reference | What was taken |
|---|---|
| A surveyor's plat sheet | The numbered plate index, the hairline rule, mono small-caps labels |
| Linear's own App Store set | The confidence to crop the device hard and let it run off the edge |
| A brass plaque on an office door | The gold-on-navy ratio, and the restraint around it |

## Type

Deliberately **not** the app's own Playfair Display + Montserrat. That pairing is
the most common "trying to look elegant" default, and Playfair's hairlines break
up on a dark ground at display size.

- **Newsreader** (variable, `opsz` exercised at 72 for display and 20 for the
  deck) carries the headlines. Lower contrast than Playfair, so it holds on navy.
- **IBM Plex Mono** carries the plate index and role labels. Drafting-table voice.
- Display tracking is negative (-0.021em); labels are positive (+0.2em). Nothing
  sits at 0.

## Colour

Sampled from `store/brand-logo-master.jpg`, not picked from a palette.

| Token | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#0D1C33` | the logo's field |
| Gold | `#C3974E` | the rock the goat stands on |
| Sand | `#F2F0EB` | the app's own paper |
| Muted | `#A2A6AB` | sand mixed 65/35 into navy |

Gold on navy measures 6.4:1, past AA for normal text. One saturated accent only,
and it appears in two places per plate.

Flat digital surfaces read as machine output, so the ground carries a 64px
drawing grid at ~4% and a real `feTurbulence` grain at 5.5% overlay.

## The copy

Every headline says something only this app could say. Swap-test each one
against a competitor's name and it stops making sense.

| Plate | Role | Headline |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Resident | Your lease, your rent, your repairs. |
| 02 | Resident | Report a leak at 11 p.m. |
| 03 | Office | Every open job, every property. |
| 04 | Office | Assign a contractor without a phone call. |
| 05 | Contractor | Contractors arrive knowing the job. |

02 is the deliberately large typographic moment (132px against 92–104px
elsewhere) because it is the shortest line and the sharpest claim: no office
hours, no phone call.

## Composition

Every plate shares the same header rule position and the same device top, so the
five read as one filmstrip when scrolled in the App Store. Variation is carried
by headline length and size, by which plates get a deck, and by the density of
the screen inside the phone: 01 medium, 02 sparse with big type, 03 dense, 04
densest, 05 calm.

**Plate 05 is the one exception**, for a real reason. The contractor's screen is
genuinely short — two jobs and a counter row — so bleeding it off the bottom
edge like the others would show half a phone of empty paper. It is cropped to
its content and shown whole, with the deck moved beneath it and the goat mark
closing the set the way a drawing set ends with a seal.

## Deviation from the anti-slop checklist

The checklist calls for breaking the grid at least twice. An earlier pass did
that with offset devices and hairline callout leaders in the right margin. Both
were removed: at this canvas ratio the margin is 84px, the leaders collided with
the device, and misaligned device tops broke the filmstrip reading. For a set
viewed as five adjacent panels rather than one scrolling page, ruthless
alignment is the correct answer and the variation belongs in the type.
